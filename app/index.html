How to use this

Play something

Copy a link, or tap Share in Kick, Twitch, YouTube or Safari, then run the Play on Pi Shortcut. You pick a TV, and it starts playing there.

You can also paste a link or type a channel name on the remote. A bare name looks on Kick; use the buttons to switch to Twitch or a YouTube search.

Pick a TV

All TVs shows a card for each TV and what it is playing. Tap one to open its remote. Controls only ever act on the TV you opened.

The remote

Pause, volume, mute and skip back or forward. Skipping is hidden on live streams. Quality changes the picture size; Refresh reloads a stream that has stalled. The power button turns the TV on or off. Stop ends playback.

Favourites and recents

Tap the star on a stream to save it as a favourite. Recents lists what was played lately. Guests can play from both but can't change favourites.

Several streams at once

The Pi's own screen can show up to four streams together. Open Multi-view on that TV's remote, then add streams. Enlarge makes one big, and Sync lines up the pictures if one is ahead.

Add it to your Home Screen

In Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It then opens like an app.

Something isn't working

Isn't live right now: the channel is offline. Nothing on the TV: check the TV is on and set to the right input, or tap the power button. Stuck or blurry: try Refresh, or pick a lower quality. Still stuck? Tell whoever set this up.

Admins: move the Pi or add a TV

Plug the Pi (or a Chromecast) into the new TV and turn that TV on. Under TVs below, tap Change or move a TV. It looks again and suggests which Roku goes with which screen, including which HDMI port the Pi is on. Fix anything that's wrong, use Test the TV, then Save. Roku TVs need Control by mobile apps set to Permissive (Settings, System, Advanced system settings).

Admins: people and guest links

People: the owner can change other admins; admins manage everyone else. Everyone logs in with a username and password. Access requests from the login page show up here to approve. Guest links let someone play and control the TVs for a set time on a set number of devices; they can't change favourites, link accounts or manage people. Revoke a link any time.

Admins: the TV screen and QR code

Between streams the Pi's screen shows a clock and a QR code that opens this remote. The TV screen card lets you hide the remote from the TV or turn the QR code off. If the Pi can't be reached after it moves, power it off and on again.

People

Access requests

Guest links

Link ready. It is shown only now, so copy it before you leave this page.

A guest can play things and control the TVs. They can't change favourites, link accounts or manage people and TVs. Each device that opens the link uses up one of its places, and it must be opened within 2 days.
